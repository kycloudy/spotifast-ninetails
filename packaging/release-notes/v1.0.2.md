Spotifast Ninetale 1.0.2 is the first release of this edition. It gives Spotifast a new look: dithered album art behind every page, a search box on Home, a floating player and an animated Ninetale wordmark. It also carries every fix made to Spotifast since 0.12.0.

Spotifast Ninetale is for Windows. Download the installer (`-setup.exe`) or the portable zip for your processor: `x86_64` for most PCs, `aarch64` for Windows on ARM.

**Download:** [all files for this release](https://github.com/kycloudy/spotifast-ninetale/releases/tag/v1.0.2)

## New

- **A new look.** Each page shows its cover, or the playing song's art, as a field of dithered dots behind its header. The dots fade as you scroll, so the page stays easy to read. The top bar is compact and names the page you are on, and the player floats as a card under the page.
- **Search on Home.** Home has its own search box, with the scopes under it and your last searches beneath. Results appear on Home itself. On other pages, search is at the top of the sidebar.
- **Your own greeting.** Click "Good evening" on Home to change it. Clear it to go back to the greeting by time of day.
- **The Ninetale wordmark** sits above the Home row on Home, drawn in dots to match the art. A band of solid dots sweeps across it while the window has focus.
- **The playing album or playlist shows Pause** on its card and its sidebar cover. By Yusuf E. (crmne/spotifast#655)
- **The Play button spins while the next song loads.** By Yusuf E. (crmne/spotifast#654)

## Fixed

- **No clicks or gaps in playback,** and sliders respond while you hold them. By Sam Boland. (crmne/spotifast#686)
- **Dragging several songs within a playlist moves them** instead of adding copies. By Sahib Kullar. (crmne/spotifast#684)
- **A song radio opened from a list is named after its song.** By kevin. (crmne/spotifast#659)
- **The Windows on ARM build matches the x64 build.** By Dennis. (crmne/spotifast#682)
- **The player's controls never overlap.** When the page beside the sidebar is too narrow for the player, the player spans the whole window.
